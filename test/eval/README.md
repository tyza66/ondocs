# Retrieval evals

The files `ondocs eval` scores in this repository's CI, each a list of questions with the pages that answer them and questions the docs should not answer ([the format](https://ondocs.vercel.app/docs/quality#score-retrieval-with-eval)).

- `docusaurus.io.yaml`: 22 questions and 6 off-topic ones about docusaurus.io's docs, written for the 2026-10-10 audit with the docs at hand. Their words are often the docs' own, so they are a best case.
- `docusaurus-title.yaml`, `astro-title.yaml` and `vitest-title.yaml`: questions people asked on Stack Overflow, in their own words, about three projects' docs, with the pages their accepted answers (or a maintainer's) link. They are a hard case, and the one your readers are closer to.

## The Stack Overflow sets

| File                    | Docs, at a pinned commit                               | Questions | Off-topic |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------: | --------: |
| `docusaurus-title.yaml` | docusaurus.io's, facebook/docusaurus@c245217, 92 pages |        33 |        15 |
| `astro-title.yaml`      | Astro's, in English, withastro/docs@3480e4b, 424 pages |        95 |        15 |
| `vitest-title.yaml`     | Vitest's, vitest-dev/vitest@2899eb3, 223 pages         |        26 |        15 |

`node scripts/corpus.mjs` fetches the three docs folders into `.corpus/`, and each file's header gives the commands that index and score it. CI does that on every change, keyword-only with no key, and fails under the thresholds in each file, which sit just under today's scores. `real-questions.test.ts` checks that every expected page is a page of the pinned docs, and that the scores are the ones [Measure and improve answers](https://ondocs.vercel.app/docs/quality#real-questions-from-stack-overflow) quotes. With an `OPENAI_API_KEY`, the [retrieval eval workflow](../../.github/workflows/retrieval-eval.yml) scores them with embeddings too, on demand.

### How the questions were chosen

From the Stack Exchange API, on 2026-10-21: every Stack Overflow question tagged `docusaurus` (515), `astrojs` (752) or `vitest` (905), and every answer to them. A question is in a set when its accepted answer, or an answer by one of the project's maintainers, links a page of the project's docs: on docusaurus.io (or v2.docusaurus.io), docs.astro.build (or astro.build/docs) or vitest.dev (or one of its version subdomains), or the page's source file in the docs repository on GitHub. No question was left out for its wording, and each is stored as its title only. The maintainers were picked out from the answerers' Stack Overflow names by an AI reviewer: Yangshun Tay and Sebastien Lorber for Docusaurus, and swithinbank (Chris Swithinbank) and Florian Lefebvre for Astro. No Vitest maintainer answered.

Most answers link no docs page, so the sets are smaller than the tags: 162 Docusaurus, 257 Astro and 292 Vitest questions have an accepted answer, and 33, 95 and 26 have an accepted or a maintainer's answer that links a page of the pinned docs.

The 15 off-topic questions of each set are the five most-voted questions of each of three tags that have nothing to do with these docs: `pandas`, `swiftui` and `postgresql` for Docusaurus, `django`, `android` and `excel` for Astro, and `ggplot2`, `kotlin` and `matlab` for Vitest.

### How the expected pages were found

A question's expected pages are the pages its chosen answers link, mapped onto the pinned docs by code, mechanically:

- A version or a language in the path is dropped: `/docs/next/…`, `/docs/2.x/…` and `/zh-CN/docs/…` are read as `/docs/…`, `/fr/guides/…` as `/en/guides/…`, and `v1.vitest.dev` as `vitest.dev`.
- A page that has moved since follows the site's own redirects, as they are at the pinned commit: docusaurus.io's `website/static/_redirects` and its client redirects, the rules in withastro/docs's `worker.js`, and vitest.dev's `docs/public/_redirects` and the redirect its theme makes of old `/config/#option` links.
- A link to a section counts as its page.

A link that still lands on no page of the pinned docs is left out, and a question none of whose links does is left out with it (two Docusaurus questions, listed below). Two links needed judgement, and an AI reviewer mapped them: each is marked "mapped by an AI reviewer" below. No person labelled these questions.

Each question has the pages of one answer, so where another page answers it as well, finding that page counts as a miss: the scores are a lower bound.
